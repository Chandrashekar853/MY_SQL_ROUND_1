Write a query to calculate the average age of employees in each department.

Solutions 1:
select d.name as "department_name",
AVG(e.age) as "average_age"
FROM employees e
join departments d
on e.department_id = d.id
group by d.name;

Output:

+-----------------+-------------+
| department_name | average_age |
+-----------------+-------------+
| IT              |     37.5500 |
| HR              |     38.4000 |
| Finance         |     41.4500 |
| Marketing       |     41.4000 |
| Operations      |     38.5500 |
+-----------------+-------------+
Write a query to find the department with the highest number of employees whose age is above 40.

solution 2:
select d.name as "department_name",
count(*) AS "employee_count"
from employees e
join
departments d
on e.department_id = d.id
where 
e.age>40
group by
d.name 
order by
employee_count desc
LIMIT 1;

Output:

+-----------------+----------------+
| department_name | employee_count |
+-----------------+----------------+
| Operations      |            101 |
+-----------------+----------------+
Write a query to retrieve the count of employees who have in joined in last 100 days.
solutions 3:
select count(*) as "employees_joined_in_last_100"
from employees
where joining_date >=current_date - interval 100 day;

Output:

+------------------------------+
| employees_joined_in_last_100 |
+------------------------------+
|                          100 |
+------------------------------+
